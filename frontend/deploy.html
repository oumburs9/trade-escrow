Deploy to Sepolia

  1. Open MetaMask and select the account you want to deploy from (your Buyer account is fine).
  2. Make sure it has a little Sepolia ETH (deploying costs well under 0.01 ETH).
  3. Click Connect, then Deploy contract, and confirm in MetaMask.
